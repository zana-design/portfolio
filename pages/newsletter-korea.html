Newsletter design

Greetings from Korea Newsletter

A photo-led editorial concept combining a cultural introduction with structured content modules.

ContextTravel / cultural storytelling
FormatEditorial email newsletter concept
FocusEditorial layout, visual hierarchy & storytelling

01 / PURPOSE

A connected editorial story.

Create an informative, welcoming newsletter concept around Korea. The bilingual greeting and architectural hero image establish the theme, while placeholder copy demonstrates how articles and supporting stories could be arranged.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

A clear visual rhythm.

A clear grid, red and navy colour blocks, and a white background establish hierarchy. The opening image anchors the page, an overlapping red panel highlights a lead story, and repeated text modules create a consistent route through the supporting content.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance expression and readability.

The hero image communicates the cultural setting before readers reach the article modules. Strong colour blocks and numbered details provide emphasis without requiring every section to use the same layout. The trade-off is competing focal points: careful alignment, restrained accents, and clear spacing keep the photograph and highlighted panels from overpowering the reading flow.

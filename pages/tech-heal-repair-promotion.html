Graphic design

Tech Heal Repair Promotion

An approachable service promotion built around a clear repair message and an illustrative visual hook.

ContextComputer and device repair
FormatPromotional flyer
FocusComposition, visual hierarchy & communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused visual message.

Introduce Tech Heal as a computer and device repair service. The artwork leads with the customer’s repair need, then presents the service offer and contact route within a compact promotional format.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

Shape, type, and image.

An illustration of technicians working on a laptop gives the service a literal, approachable expression. A radiating background draws attention to the headline, while layered blue shapes organise the supporting service information.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance expression and clarity.

The repair illustration makes the offer understandable before the reader reaches the details. The playful visual language lowers the barrier to a technical service, while the prominent pickup-and-delivery message gives the audience a practical reason to respond. The trade-off is visual activity: supporting text needs sufficient contrast and space against the patterned background.

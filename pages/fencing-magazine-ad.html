Magazine advertisement

Australian Fencing Magazine Ad

A rural access-control story bringing Palgate and LionHead Locks together in one CSM advertisement.

BrandsCSM, Palgate & LionHead Locks
FormatSingle-page magazine advertisement
FocusRural access, product presentation & print communication

01 / PURPOSE

Connect the product to the setting.

Introduce smart gate and door access for rural, agricultural, and commercial properties. The advertisement brings Palgate automation and LionHead locking products into one offer, led by the benefit of controlling access remotely.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

A shared application story.

A rural gate photograph establishes the context before the product group introduces the range. Bold white headings provide a clear entry point, while short product labels explain the role of each device. The brand strip and contact footer connect the two product families to CSM.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance range and readability.

The setting gives the hardware a practical purpose rather than presenting it in isolation. Grouping the products supports the idea of a connected access solution. With several devices and brands sharing one page, the critical trade-off is density: clear label placement, contrast against the photograph, and space around the contact details help readers find a route through the information.

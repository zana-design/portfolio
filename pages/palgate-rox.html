01 / OBJECTIVE

Build meaningful visibility.

Strengthen Palgate’s visibility in the Australian access-control and fencing industries. The campaign aimed to build brand recognition, support sales activity, and establish a stronger digital presence.

02 / MY ROLE

From planning to execution.

Marketing Graphic Designer & Campaign Lead, coordinating a multi-channel strategy across website content, LinkedIn scheduling, brand communications, industry promotion, and product marketing assets.

03 / APPROACH

Connect the channels.

A LinkedIn schedule every three days brought product highlights, updates, and industry content into a consistent rhythm. Website design and content structure supported clearer product presentation, while Australian Fencing Awards Yearbook 2025 promotion added a relevant industry touchpoint.

04 / DESIGN RATIONALE

Recognition with a clear message.

A recurring blue palette and visible product imagery connect different formats to the Palgate brand. Real installations make the product context concrete; concise headlines and supporting feature content serve both quick social browsing and more detailed product exploration. Trade promotion places the brand in front of a relevant professional audience.

05 / CRITICAL REASONING

Balance impact with substance.

Consistency is useful when each post contributes a different reason to engage. Repeating the same product render and headline risks making the feed feel interchangeable. Mixing installation context, practical use cases, product details, and brand stories gives the visual system variety. Yearbook exposure supports visibility, but it should not be presented as evidence that the product won an award.

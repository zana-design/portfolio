01 / PURPOSE

A clear introduction.

Explain Palgate as a connected access-control platform rather than a collection of separate devices. The brochure introduces the ecosystem, use cases, app, and hardware before presenting product-specific information.

02 / AUDIENCE

Designed for the reader.

Customers and security professionals exploring gates, doors, intercoms, and vehicle access. The content connects the wider platform story to the individual features and specifications needed for closer evaluation.

03 / LAYOUT

A consistent reading order.

The cover links hardware to an access-control setting. Inside, blue accents, clear product headings, feature icons, related product codes, and specification tables create a consistent progression from overview to detail.

04 / DESIGN RATIONALE

Why this visual direction.

Showing the system in context gives the product range a shared purpose. Feature icons make the first scan more approachable, while tables and code lists serve readers who need precise detail. The blue-and-white system connects the brochure to the Palgate identity without competing with product imagery.

05 / CRITICAL REASONING

Clarity before decoration.

A platform brochure has to serve readers with different levels of technical knowledge. Separating application benefits from specifications keeps the first read accessible, while retaining detail for professional evaluation. A consistent product structure also helps prevent visually prominent devices from overshadowing less familiar parts of the ecosystem.

Flyer design

Elite Alarms Siren Flyer

A bold product-led promotion for the Elite external siren and strobe.

BrandElite Alarms
FormatSingle-page promotional flyer
FocusVisual hierarchy, product presentation & promotional communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused message.

Present the EC-SIREN-B as the featured product while showing the white edition as an alternative. The original promotion pairs the product range with a quantity offer and a compact row of supporting product information.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

A clear visual order.

A dark background and blue accents frame large front and side product views. The headline sits beside the main product, with the alternative finish placed in a separate lower section so the two messages stay distinct.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance impact and clarity.

Showing two angles makes the form and strobe detail easier to understand. The darker setting gives the blue accent a clear role, while the white edition introduces a useful contrast. Maintaining visible edges around the black product is the key trade-off: atmosphere should not hide the shape being promoted.

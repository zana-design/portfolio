Graphic design

Logo Design Collection

A collection of distinctive marks exploring symbols, letterforms, and brand personality.

ContextIdentity design
FormatLogo and symbol collection
FocusComposition, visual hierarchy & communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused visual message.

Bring a range of logo designs into one collection, spanning technology, photography, food, packaging, professional services, and sport. The marks explore different ways to connect a name or service with a memorable visual signature.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

Shape, type, and image.

The collection combines monograms, pictorial symbols, custom lettering, and wordmarks. Each uses a different balance of shape, colour, and typography, from restrained single-colour marks to more expressive illustrative identities.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance expression and clarity.

A logo needs to communicate with a small number of recognisable elements. Combining a relevant symbol with a clear name can make the identity easier to interpret, while distinctive proportions help it stand apart. The critical trade-off is detail versus flexibility: marks need to retain their character at small sizes and across light, dark, and single-colour applications.

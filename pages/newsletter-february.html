Newsletter design

The Secure Scoop — February 2026

Company news, product highlights, and people stories brought together in one editorial system.

ContextCSM
FormatThree-page company newsletter
FocusEditorial layout, visual hierarchy & storytelling

01 / PURPOSE

A connected editorial story.

Connect CSM news and announcements with product updates and team stories. The February issue moves from company developments to smart-home entry kits, employee milestones, and a personal spotlight.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

A clear visual rhythm.

A red, black, and white palette gives the issue a clear CSM identity. Large section headings divide longer stories, while photography, product comparisons, and highlighted panels introduce different reading rhythms across the three pages.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance expression and readability.

The modular structure allows business updates, technical content, and people-led stories to feel part of the same publication. Repeating the masthead and section styles supports continuity. The key trade-off is density: product explanations and longer employee features need enough spacing and hierarchy to remain inviting.

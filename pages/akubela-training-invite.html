Akubela Training Invitation

An event-led invitation to an Akubela product introduction session.

BrandAkubela / CSM
FormatDigital event invitation
FocusVisual hierarchy, product presentation & promotional communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused message.

Invite customers and partners to a live Microsoft Teams introduction to the Akubela ecosystem. The artwork presents the session’s practical value through product knowledge, sales positioning, setup guidance, and direct support.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

A clear visual order.

A black background, orange headings, and outlined display type give the invitation a clear event identity. Product images frame the content, while the date, time, and RSVP information create a defined response path within the original artwork.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance impact and clarity.

The invitation needs to answer three questions quickly: what the session is, when it takes place, and why it is useful. Contrasting headings organise the longer description and benefit list. The key trade-off is decorative scale versus practical detail: large display text should leave the schedule and RSVP information easy to find.

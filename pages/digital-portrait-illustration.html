Graphic design

Digital Portrait Illustration

A graphic portrait using a limited palette, defined shapes, and expressive contrast.

ContextIllustration & visual expression
FormatStylised digital portrait
FocusComposition, visual hierarchy & communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused visual message.

Develop a stylised portrait with a strong visual identity. The masked face and cyan hood establish a compact composition focused on expression, silhouette, and colour contrast.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

Shape, type, and image.

Flat areas of colour and sharp contour changes define the face, hair, and clothing. Cyan highlights separate the hood from the darker purple and black tones, while the background motif supports the central figure.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance expression and clarity.

With much of the face covered, the eyes and surrounding shapes carry the expression. The limited palette unifies the portrait, and the bright hood creates immediate separation from the background. The main trade-off is emphasis: supporting symbols and highlights should reinforce the face rather than compete with it.

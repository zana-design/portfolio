01 / PURPOSE

A clear introduction.

Bring the LionHead Locks range into one professional catalogue. It introduces the brand, access technologies, and TTLock integration before moving into product families, dimensions, compatibility, and supporting information.

02 / AUDIENCE

Designed for the reader.

Locksmiths and installers comparing locking products. The layout gives prominence to model names, finish variants, access methods, and compatible lock bodies so readers can move from recognition to detailed selection.

03 / LAYOUT

A consistent reading order.

The black-and-gold cover establishes the brand, while the product pages use a repeatable information structure. Photography, access-method icons, specification blocks, and datasheet links give each model a familiar reading order.

04 / DESIGN RATIONALE

Why this visual direction.

A repeated product template reduces the effort of finding the same detail across different models. Gold headings connect the internal pages to the cover without making every surface dark. Product images remain prominent, but the information hierarchy gives installers practical details alongside the visual presentation.

05 / CRITICAL REASONING

Clarity before decoration.

A large range creates pressure to fit too much into each page. Consistent spacing, legible codes, and a clear distinction between included components and compatible accessories matter more than decorative variety. Product imagery and catalogue summaries should support selection alongside the official datasheets, rather than replace them.

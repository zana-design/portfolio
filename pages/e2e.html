01 / OBJECTIVE

Build meaningful visibility.

Build visibility for E2E Thermal while helping customers understand the product range and its applications. The campaign connected launch awareness with educational content and resources for sales and technical teams.

02 / MY ROLE

From planning to execution.

Marketing Graphic Designer & Campaign Lead, responsible for end-to-end execution: positioning, website content, social campaigns, marketing collateral, launch communications, and sales enablement materials.

03 / APPROACH

Connect the channels.

A structured weekly social schedule combined product highlights, feature explanations, and use cases. Brochures, promotional assets, and sales and technical training materials extended the same message across customer touchpoints.

04 / DESIGN RATIONALE

Recognition with a clear message.

The recurring orange product imagery creates a visual anchor across the campaign. Short, benefit-led headlines introduce the subject quickly, while feature-led materials give professional audiences more detail. Using both formats supports an introduction to the product and a more considered follow-up conversation.

05 / CRITICAL REASONING

Balance impact with substance.

Fire imagery creates urgency, but it can overwhelm the product message if it dominates every asset. Pairing it with clear product views, readable hierarchy, and explanatory material keeps the communication grounded in the solution. Technical statements need supporting product documentation; the visual campaign should introduce those claims clearly without replacing the specifications.

OUTCOMES & NEXT STEPS

A foundation to build on.

Reported outcomes

  • Increased awareness of the E2E Thermal range across digital channels.
  • Improved engagement with targeted launch and social content.
  • Stronger customer and partner understanding of thermal fire detection.
  • A reusable framework for future product promotions.

The campaign presentation reports these outcomes qualitatively; it does not provide numerical performance measures.

Moving forward

Continue regular social publishing, develop product and website content, and add real-world installations and customer-focused case studies.

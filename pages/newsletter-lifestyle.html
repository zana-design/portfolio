Newsletter design

Lifestyle Newsletter

A warm editorial layout balancing a human introduction, service highlights, and featured stories.

ContextLifestyle / hospitality
FormatEmail newsletter concept
FocusEditorial layout, visual hierarchy & storytelling

01 / PURPOSE

A connected editorial story.

Explore an approachable newsletter layout for lifestyle, food, or hospitality content. The source uses placeholder copy to demonstrate the visual structure, rather than a completed editorial campaign.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

A clear visual rhythm.

Cream, orange, and plum establish a warm palette with clear contrast between sections. A portrait-led opening introduces the page, followed by icon-based highlights, a large supporting photograph, and smaller image-and-text story blocks.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance expression and readability.

Different content formats give the page a useful rhythm while the repeated colours keep it coherent. Icons make the highlights easy to scan, and photography adds context. The critical balance is between rich colour and readability: longer text needs quieter backgrounds and generous spacing so the visual energy does not overwhelm the content.

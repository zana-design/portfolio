Flyer design

TVT Acusearch Flyer

A clear introduction to AI-assisted footage search and camera alerts.

BrandTVT
FormatSingle-page product flyer
FocusVisual hierarchy, product presentation & promotional communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused message.

Introduce TVT Acusearch through the practical benefit of reducing footage-review effort. The flyer connects that message to camera and NVR imagery, firmware information, push-notification features, and the original promotional price.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

A clear visual order.

A large headline establishes the benefit first. Blue highlights connect the product name, firmware information, and notification section, while the circular price panel provides a separate promotional focal point.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance impact and clarity.

The page separates the main benefit from the supporting compatibility and update details, giving readers a clear route into more technical content. The price needs prominence, but should not overpower the product explanation. Strong alignment and consistent feature icons help the different information levels remain connected.

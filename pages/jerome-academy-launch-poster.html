Graphic design

Jerome Academy Launch Poster

A launch announcement balancing sporting energy with clear registration information.

ContextBadminton academy
FormatOpening promotion poster
FocusComposition, visual hierarchy & communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused visual message.

Announce Jerome Badminton Academy’s opening and introduce its coaching, membership, and booking offer. The poster brings the opening message, introductory promotion, facilities, and contact details into one visual hierarchy.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

Shape, type, and image.

The academy’s navy-and-red identity connects the poster to its logo. Court and equipment photography establish the setting, while a large opening headline, coloured offer strip, and grouped information blocks guide the reader through the announcement.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance expression and clarity.

The hierarchy prioritises the opening first, followed by the offer and ways to participate. Separating the service list from the facility details makes the dense information easier to scan. At poster scale, the challenge is balancing impact at a distance with smaller practical details, especially the registration and contact information.

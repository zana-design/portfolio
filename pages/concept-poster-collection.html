Graphic design

Concept Poster Collection

Three poster concepts exploring symbolism, atmosphere, and a single dominant visual idea.

ContextFilm-inspired & visual storytelling
FormatConceptual poster studies
FocusComposition, visual hierarchy & communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused visual message.

Explore how a poster can suggest a story through a focused visual metaphor. The collection brings the Kung Fu Panda, Titanic, and Alone concepts together as a set of composition and storytelling studies.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

Shape, type, and image.

The blossoming tree, submerged necklace, and isolated astronaut provide distinct focal points. Colour, negative space, lighting, and title placement give each poster its own mood while keeping the composition centred on one primary idea.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance expression and clarity.

Reducing a narrative to one memorable image can create a stronger first impression than adding multiple competing scenes. The tree uses a lighter symbolic approach, the necklace introduces tension through darkness, and the astronaut builds a sense of isolation. Typography needs to support those moods without weakening the focal image or becoming difficult to read.

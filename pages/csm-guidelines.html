01 / PURPOSE

A dependable identity.

CSM supplies electronic security solutions to professionals across Australia. The guide expresses its focus on trust, technical expertise, and partnership through a clear, repeatable visual language.

02 / APPROACH

Strong foundations.

The system defines logo variations, appropriate usage, clear space, typography, and colour. These shared foundations keep formal communications and promotional materials recognisably connected.

03 / APPLICATION

Consistency in practice.

The peripherals translate the system into stationery, apparel, drinkware, and accessories. Black and white establish the base, with red accents and recurring CSM graphics tying the collection together.

LOGO EVOLUTION

Clarity that carries the heritage forward.

The guide presents a move from the original, detailed identity to a cleaner CSM wordmark. The purpose is to retain strength and trust while making the identity more adaptable to contemporary security communications.

VISUAL FOUNDATIONS

Impact, balanced with readability.

Bison & Montserrat

Bison gives headlines a bold, geometric voice. Montserrat supports body copy and digital applications with a more open, versatile character. Their different roles create hierarchy without making every line compete for attention.

Black, white & vivid red

The primary palette keeps communication direct and high in contrast. Red adds a controlled point of emphasis; supporting greys give layouts depth without introducing a competing colour language.

DESIGN REASONING

A consistent system with room to adapt.

The guide distinguishes between formal logo lockups and the simpler mark used in promotional contexts. This keeps official communication informative while allowing smaller applications to stay clear. The practical trade-off is detail versus legibility: taglines and repeated patterns need enough space and contrast to remain readable on different materials. Consistency comes from preserving the core identity, rather than putting every brand element on every object.

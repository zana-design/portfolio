Oakfire Burgers

Building a warm, welcoming burger brand around the idea of the hearth.

01. THE BRIEF

Creating a distinctive identity that feels warm, crafted and memorable.

Oakfire Burgers needed a brand that communicates fire, craft and hospitality without becoming aggressive or overly literal. The direction centred on warmth, friendliness, welcoming hospitality and the feeling of gathering around a hearth.

WEBSITE DESIGN CASE STUDY

Case Study

Plug IT — A clear, approachable website for a technology solutions provider.

Case studyPurpose & conceptAudienceApproachChallengesKey featuresUser journeyVisual systemVisual designResult

01 / THE BRIEF

Purpose & Concept

The objective was to design a clean, professional, and conversion-focused website for a technology solutions provider, communicating innovation, reliability, and user-centric service. Targeted at both small businesses and large enterprises, the site highlights services like IT consulting, cloud integration, and managed support, with intuitive navigation, scalability, and strong calls to action to drive engagement and leads.

The design challenge

Plug IT brings together a broad range of technical and creative services. The website needed to make that breadth easy to understand, establish a recognisable brand, and give visitors a clear route from discovering a service to starting a conversation.

The solution

A focused landing page introduces the brand with an illustrated hero, explains its customer commitments, organises services into a consistent card grid, and closes with a direct enquiry section. Navy provides structure, orange draws attention to actions, and generous whitespace keeps the content approachable.

02 / WHO IT SERVES

Target Audience

The project brief identifies small businesses and large enterprises as the core audience. The page supports both a visitor seeking one specific service and a decision-maker exploring a longer-term technology partner.

Small business owners

Looking for practical help with a website, hosting, customer management, or ongoing support.

What the page needs to answer

  • Which service matches my business need?
  • Can I understand the offering without technical jargon?
  • How do I ask a question or request a quote?

Design response

Clearly named service cards, approachable illustrations, and a short enquiry form create a straightforward path to contact.

Enterprise decision-makers

Exploring technical capabilities, consultancy, and a reliable service relationship.

What the page needs to answer

  • What capabilities does Plug IT offer?
  • How does the brand communicate reliability and support?
  • Where can I start a discussion about a wider requirement?

Design response

A professional visual system, a dedicated values section, and an organised overview of services support an initial evaluation.

03 / DESIGN APPROACH

Lead with the brand. Keep the experience clear.

The design strategy centered around a modern, responsive layout built on a clean white and blue color scheme—instilling a sense of trust and technical precision. Strategic use of orange for buttons and icons introduced contrast and directed user attention toward interactive elements such as CTAs (“Get Started”) and contact forms.

The landing page opens with a strong hero section featuring bold illustrations that reflect collaboration, technology, and forward-thinking. Large headings and concise copy establish clarity and intent within seconds of landing. Below the fold, the content is broken into easily scannable sections—highlighting key offerings, service categories, and value propositions.

Custom vector illustrations were integrated throughout the site to convey complex technical services in a more relatable and approachable way. The use of iconography helps to break up content-heavy areas, maintaining visual interest without overwhelming the user.

DESIGN FOCUS

Brand translation · Page hierarchy · Service presentation · Responsive UI · Visual design · Calls to action · Enquiry flow

04 / DESIGN CONSIDERATIONS

Make technical services feel approachable.

  • A broad service offering. Hosting, development, consultancy, CRM, graphic design, and gap analysis need a consistent presentation so visitors can scan the range without losing their place.
  • Different levels of technical confidence. Clear labels and relevant illustrations help explain the offering to both business owners and technical stakeholders.
  • Brand expression and readability. The bulb illustration gives the hero a distinctive character, while whitespace separates the artwork from the headline and primary action.
  • A clear next step. The prominent “Get Started” action and the “Let’s Talk” section make enquiry a visible part of the page journey.
  • Consistency across devices. Spacing, typography, and content hierarchy need to remain readable as the layout moves from desktop to smaller screens.

06 / PAGE FLOW

A clear user journey.

The visual hierarchy guides attention from introductory content to service offerings, and finally to lead capture. Each section answers a different question before asking visitors to take the next step.

  1. 01 / DISCOVER

    Understand the offer

    The hero introduces Plug IT and its focus on innovation and reliable solutions.

  2. 02 / BUILD CONFIDENCE

    Meet the values

    Customer commitments explain the service relationship the brand wants to communicate.

  3. 03 / EXPLORE

    Find a service

    The card grid gives visitors a scannable overview of the available capabilities.

  4. 04 / CONNECT

    Start a conversation

    The contact section provides a direct way to describe a requirement or request a quote.

07 / VISUAL SYSTEM

Professional, clear, and recognisable.

The supplied design uses navy and blue to establish a technical, professional foundation, orange to highlight actions, and light backgrounds to create breathing room. The illustration style adds warmth to the technology story.

Navy & blue

Structure, service labels, and the values section.

Orange

Primary actions, section accents, and iconography.

Light neutrals

Open backgrounds and separation between content groups.

Typography & spacing

Bold sans-serif headings establish a clear hierarchy. Supporting copy is smaller and more restrained, while generous spacing separates the headline, illustration, service cards, and enquiry area.

Illustration & iconography

The central bulb represents ideas and innovation. People, devices, and service-specific illustrations connect the technical offering to practical business needs.

08 / THE WEBSITE

Visual Design

The original project artwork brings together the illustrated hero, customer commitments, service grid, and contact section in the navy-and-orange identity.

09 / RESULT

A bold brand, made easy to navigate.

The final design combines a distinctive illustrated introduction with structured service content and a direct route to enquiry. The desktop and mobile presentation keeps the brand recognisable while giving the offering a clear reading order.

Its conversion focus comes through in the placement of service enquiries, contact information, and calls to action. The result is a visual direction designed to communicate expertise, make technical services easier to understand, and encourage prospective clients to start a conversation.

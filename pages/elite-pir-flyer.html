Flyer design

Elite Alarms PIR Flyer

A restrained product promotion centred on the EC-PIR detector.

BrandElite Alarms
FormatSingle-page promotional flyer
FocusVisual hierarchy, product presentation & promotional communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused message.

Give the EC-PIR detector a clear promotional focus. The original flyer combines the product with a quantity offer and three feature cues: intelligent detection, rotatable lens technology, and pet-friendly protection.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

A clear visual order.

A large central product image gives the detector visual priority. Blue typography, a quiet background, and a compact offer panel create a clean hierarchy, with feature icons supporting a quick scan along the bottom.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance impact and clarity.

The minimal composition allows a visually simple white product to remain the subject. The framed offer provides a second focal point without competing with a dense body of copy. Because the product and background are both light, edge definition and tonal contrast are important to preserve the detector’s shape.

Flyer design

Akubela Smart Home Flyer

A benefit-led introduction to the Akubela smart home entry kit.

BrandAkubela
FormatSingle-page product flyer
FocusVisual hierarchy, product presentation & promotional communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused message.

Introduce the door phone, HyPanel Lite, and BelaHome app as a connected entry solution for homes, apartments, and retrofit projects. The flyer translates the kit into everyday benefits such as remote answering, keyless access, and scene control.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

A clear visual order.

A prominent kit heading establishes the offer, followed by a clear product composition and short benefit blocks. Orange icons and headings link the content to the Akubela identity, while a light background supports reading.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance impact and clarity.

The product group makes the relationship between the hardware and app visible before the supporting copy explains it. Separate benefit blocks give readers several entry points. The main trade-off is information density: consistent spacing and concise headings keep the single page from feeling like a technical manual.

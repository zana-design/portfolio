Graphic design

Social Media Creative Collection

Awareness posts, brand stories, recruitment, and seasonal greetings brought together as one collection.

ContextFood, research, education & brand communication
FormatSocial media design collection
FocusComposition, visual hierarchy & communication

01 / PURPOSE

A focused visual message.

Create concise visual messages for different social audiences, including food awareness, product development, research, education, recruitment, and seasonal communication. The related social media boards are combined here so the work can be explored as one project.

02 / DESIGN APPROACH

Shape, type, and image.

Food photography anchors awareness-day and ingredient posts, while conceptual imagery and type-led compositions support research and education themes. Recurring brand marks and footer treatments give each organisation a consistent presence across varied topics.

03 / DESIGN REASONING

Balance expression and clarity.

A social post needs an immediate point of focus before supporting detail. Image scale, strong headlines, and restrained copy establish that first read. Variation keeps the collection engaging, but consistency still matters: logos, spacing, and message hierarchy should remain recognisable as subject matter and seasonal palettes change.
